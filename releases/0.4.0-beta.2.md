# Pinna 0.4.0-beta.2

You can add your own wake words on the dashboard: a phrase and its openWakeWord model. The satellite tests each model before it keeps it, and Home Assistant offers the new words like the built-in ones.

## Before you update

This is a beta: it goes to satellites whose **Updates** setting is `beta`.

## Highlights

### Add your own wake word

Open **Voice & sound** > **Wake words** to add up to eight wake words of your
own. Enter the phrase and choose an openWakeWord model, an `.onnx` or
`.tflite` file of at most 2 MiB. The satellite tests the model in a process of
its own before it keeps it: the model must load, run fast enough, and stay
quiet on silence and noise. The test takes up to a minute. If the satellite
refuses the model, the page says why, and nothing is kept. Pinna can't run
microWakeWord models. The new word isn't active yet: choose it like the
built-in words, on **Voice & sound** or, in Home Assistant mode, on the device
page in Home Assistant. **Remove** next to a word deletes its model. See
[Add your own wake word](https://getpinna.dev/docs/dashboard/#add-your-own-wake-word).

## New in Home Assistant

- The **Wake word** and **Wake word 2** selectors list the words you added.
  After you add or remove one, the satellite makes Home Assistant reconnect
  once nothing plays, and the selectors show the new list.

## Other changes

- A word you added whose model is missing, changed or doesn't load shows the
  reason on **Wake words**. You can't choose it, and Home Assistant isn't
  offered it.

## All changes

Changes since 0.4.0-beta.1.

### New

- Test an uploaded wake word model on silence, noise and time (pinna-wakeword)
- The device can replace the wake words it offers (pinna-esphome)
- Owners add and remove custom wake words, tested in a process of their own (pinnad)
- Add and remove custom wake words (dashboard)
