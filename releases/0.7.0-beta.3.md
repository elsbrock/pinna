# Pinna 0.7.0-beta.3

Satellites in Home Assistant mode take messages and control from their satellite group, except timers. The device box on the dashboard opens into a shorter satellite list. The Echo (2nd gen) plays its bass below 150 Hz.

## Before you update

This is a beta: it goes to satellites whose **Updates** setting is `beta`.

- The Echo Dot (2nd gen) and the Echo (2nd gen) get a rebuilt kernel with a
  new Wi-Fi fix for group key rekeys. It has not run on a satellite yet. The
  Echo Dot (5th gen) gets a rebuilt kernel too. If the new version doesn't
  start, the satellite goes back to the previous one by itself.
- The image no longer has `vim`, `evtest`, the `openssl` command, or BlueZ's
  `btmon`, `gatttool`, `sdptool`, `obexctl` and `mpris-proxy`. Nothing on the
  dashboard uses them; this matters only if you work on the satellite over
  SSH.

## Highlights

### Satellites in Home Assistant mode join in the group

A satellite in Home Assistant mode now takes messages from the other members
of its satellite group, and runs what they ask of it: the volume, the reply
volume, the ring, music and radio. It refuses timers, because Home Assistant
sets its timers. It speaks messages with its own text-to-speech service
under **Assistant > Services**, which has to pass its check. It sends no
messages itself: you still ask a satellite in standalone mode. The
**Group messages** and **Control from the group** switches under
**Assistant > Satellite group** turn this off. See
[Satellite groups](https://getpinna.dev/docs/satellite-groups/).

### A shorter satellite list

On the dashboard, the device box in the sidebar now opens into the list of
satellites. Each satellite shows its drawing and its name only, with this
satellite at the bottom. The satellite search also finds the other
satellites more reliably: it asks three times, and keeps a satellite listed
while its dashboard still answers.

## Other changes

- The image is about 5.5 MB smaller, which leaves the Echo Dot (5th gen)
  about 31 MB free in its system slot.

## Fixed

- The Echo (2nd gen) plays its woofer's range from 40 Hz. The speaker took
  away everything under 150 Hz, which suits only the Echo Dot's small
  driver.
- The Echo Dot (2nd gen) and the Echo (2nd gen) keep receiving broadcasts
  on Wi-Fi after the access point changes its group key. Before, an access
  point that rekeys often, such as a FRITZ!Box, could cut off DHCP, ARP and
  mDNS until the satellite rejoined.
- The wake word is caught more easily while the satellite plays music or
  speaks.
- Satellites of one group list each other reliably. Before, a satellite
  could miss another one for minutes.
- On the Echo Dot (5th gen), Wi-Fi sends with one spatial stream, as its
  antenna allows: fewer retries and less packet loss on 5 GHz.
- With **Control from the group** on in Home Assistant mode, the hint no
  longer says the group controls this satellite's timers.

## All changes

Changes since 0.7.0-beta.2.

### New

- The device box opens into the satellite list, which names each satellite only (dashboard)
- A group member in Home Assistant mode takes messages and control, except timers (pinnad)
- The group page says what a member in Home Assistant mode takes (dashboard)
- The root image leaves out tools and modules nothing on the satellite uses (image)

### Fixed

- The Echo (2nd gen) keeps its woofer's range under 150 Hz (pinna-audio)
- Wi-Fi keeps receiving broadcasts after a group key rekey (kernel)
- The wake word takes one supporting score while the satellite plays (pinnad)
- The satellite search sends each query three times and keeps a satellite whose dashboard answers (pinnad)
- Control from the group leaves timers out in Home Assistant mode (dashboard)
- The Echo Dot (5th gen)'s Wi-Fi sends one spatial stream (kernel)
