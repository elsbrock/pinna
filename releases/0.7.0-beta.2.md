# Pinna 0.7.0-beta.2

The installer in your browser installs the Echo Dot (5th gen) from TWRP. System has a Timeline page: while debugging runs, it shows what the satellite does, every request it sends, and the audio levels on one time axis.

## Before you update

This is a beta: it goes to satellites whose **Updates** setting is `beta`.

- The Echo Dot (5th gen) gets a rebuilt kernel, with the wireless extensions
  for the Wi-Fi signal metric. If the new version doesn't start, the
  satellite goes back to the previous one by itself.

## Highlights

### Install the Echo Dot (5th gen) from the browser

The [installer](https://getpinna.dev/install/) now has the Echo Dot (5th
gen) next to the 2nd gen Echos. After firebreak's unlock, start TWRP with
**Volume Up**, connect the test pads' USB, and the page does what
`install-echo-dot-5.sh` does: it checks the device, writes `boot_a` and
`system_a`, starts slot A on trial, and restarts. TWRP in slot B and
`userdata` stay. The page downloads this release for it, and checks the tool
it runs in TWRP, `pinna-bootctl`, against the release's signature. See
[Install on the Echo Dot (5th gen)](https://getpinna.dev/docs/flash/#install-on-the-echo-dot-5th-gen).
The script still works too.

### A timeline of what the satellite does

**System** › **Timeline** turns debugging on for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8
hours. It draws the satellite's phases, wake words and near misses, each
stage of a request, and every request it sends to a service, on one time
axis, and lists the requests in a table. With a dashboard password it also
draws the audio levels: the microphones before and after the gain, each wake
word's score, and the speaker. The text of the requests needs a press of
**Action** on the satellite as well. The satellite keeps the last 10
minutes; **Download** saves them as a file for an issue.

Nothing is recorded until you start it, and **Stop** clears what it kept.
While it runs, **Overview** says so. See
[Follow a request on the timeline](https://getpinna.dev/docs/troubleshooting/#follow-a-request-on-the-timeline).

## New in Home Assistant

- **Debugging** (binary sensor): on while debugging runs on the satellite.
  You can only read it; debugging starts and stops on the dashboard.

## Fixed

- On the Echo Dot (5th gen), joining Wi-Fi after the setup hotspot gets an
  address again. The hotspot left its interface on the radio, and no DHCP
  answer arrived.
- On the Echo Dot (5th gen), the speaker no longer drops out two or three
  times a minute.
- The Echo Dot (5th gen) reports its Wi-Fi signal to Prometheus.
- The log no longer fills with "bluetooth: running with the Wi-Fi up" every
  31 seconds while Bluetooth runs.
- Starting Bluetooth no longer logs a systemctl failure under AirPlay's name
  on every boot.
- A restart no longer logs that the AirPlay receiver or the Bluetooth bridge
  stopped.

## All changes

Changes since 0.7.0-beta.1.

### New

- A traced send reports each request's timing, size and outcome (pinna-http)
- Read-only binary sensors, and a Debugging sensor (pinna-esphome)
- Debugging keeps a 10-minute timeline of requests, for a time the dashboard sets (pinnad)
- Debugging records the phases, wake words, runs and their stages (pinnad)
- Debugging records the satellite group's calls (pinnad)
- System > Timeline shows requests and the audio pipeline on one time axis (dashboard)
- Read each word's latest score (pinna-wakeword)
- The speaker can report its output level (pinna-audio)
- Debugging records the audio levels in 100 ms buckets (pinnad)
- The signed manifest names pinna-bootctl for the browser installer (image)

### Fixed

- The setup hotspot takes ap0 off the Echo Dot (5th gen)'s radio when it stops (image)
- The button watcher opens an input device without buttons only once (pinna-hal)
- The Echo Dot (5th gen) kernel has /proc/net/wireless, for the Wi-Fi signal metric (kernel)
- A Bluetooth start counts once, not every 31 s while it runs (pinnad)
- Starting Bluetooth no longer logs a systemctl failure on every boot (pinnad)
- A reboot no longer logs that the AirPlay receiver or the Bluetooth bridge stopped (pinnad)
- The debugging timeline holds less memory and keeps the Brain told (pinnad)
- A chat answer that ended with [DONE] is not a stopped request (pinna-assist)
- The timeline's bands stand apart and the page scrolls with its time axis in view (dashboard)
- The debugging timeline names wake words by their phrase, not their id (pinnad)
- What debugging kept ages out on time, and a request open at its end is followed to it (pinnad)
- The speaker's output level is stamped with the time it is heard (pinna-audio)
- The audio levels and the text don't start while the microphones are off (pinnad)
- The timeline shows what you select, asks before Stop clears it, and keeps its last minutes in view after it ends (dashboard)
