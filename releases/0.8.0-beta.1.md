# Pinna 0.8.0-beta.1

The satellite can recognize who speaks: enroll each person on the new People page, and in standalone mode each one gets their own instructions. The daily update check names the hardware for public statistics, unless you turn that off.

## Before you update

This is a beta: it goes to satellites whose **Updates** setting is `beta`.

- The satellite's daily update check now names its hardware by codename (`biscuit`, `radar` or `cannoli`), next to the version and the channel it already sent. The release service counts it with the country, for the public [statistics](https://getpinna.dev/statistics/); the count holds no address or identifier. This is on by default. To leave the statistics, turn off **Include in the statistics** on the dashboard's **System > Updates** page, or the switch of the same name in Home Assistant. See [Include in the statistics](https://getpinna.dev/docs/update/#include-in-the-statistics).
- The wake word now runs on a newer tract, 0.23.10. It scores the test recordings as before; so far it has run on an Echo Dot (2nd gen) only.
- The image is about 16 MB bigger, for the speaker model and the new tract. The Echo Dot (5th gen) keeps about 15 MB free in its system slot.

## Highlights

### The satellite recognizes who speaks

Add the people in your household under **Voice & sound > People**. Each person presses Action at the satellite and reads for 30 seconds; the satellite keeps a voiceprint, never the recording. After that it compares each spoken request with those voices. In standalone mode, a recognized person's requests get the extra instructions you wrote for them, and the brain is told their name; everyone the satellite doesn't recognize gets the instructions of **Everyone else**. When someone else speaks during a conversation, a new conversation starts; **Continue the conversation when someone else speaks** under **Assistant > Conversation** keeps it instead. Recognition is a convenience, not a lock: put restrictions on **Everyone else**. Voiceprints stay on the satellite, and with nobody enrolled the speaker model isn't loaded. See [Recognize who speaks](https://getpinna.dev/docs/people/).

### The ring shows when the setup hotspot runs

A satellite with no saved Wi-Fi network shows a white arc at the front of the ring, slowly getting brighter and dimmer, while its setup hotspot runs. It shows once the boot stages have faded. The **Voice & sound > Ring** page explains it.

## New in Home Assistant

- **Last speaker** (text sensor): who spoke in the last spoken request, or "Everyone else". It is empty while a request starts. Home Assistant keeps its history.
- **Include in the statistics** (switch, configuration): the same setting as on the dashboard's **System > Updates** page.

## Fixed

- A satellite name longer than the dashboard's device box ends in an ellipsis instead of wrapping, and shows in full on hover.
