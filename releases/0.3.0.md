# Pinna 0.3.0

The first release of Pinna for the Echo Dot (2nd gen) and the Echo (2nd gen). It is a voice satellite for Home Assistant or on its own, set up from a dashboard in your browser, with signed updates that go back when they fail.

## Before you update

These points matter if the satellite runs a build of your own.

- Release images carry no SSH key. If you log in to the satellite over SSH
  with the key from your build's `secrets/authorized_keys`, copy it on the
  satellite to `/var/lib/pinna/ssh/authorized_keys` before you update.
  Otherwise SSH refuses it afterwards. Wi-Fi, the name and the Home Assistant
  key stay: they are kept in `/var`.
- A satellite whose build is signed with a key of your own (`just keygen`)
  refuses this release. Install it over fastboot instead, as described in
  [Install Pinna](https://elsbrock.github.io/pinna/docs/flash/).
- A satellite built before 17 November 2026 has no **Updates** setting and
  keeps an older slot record. See
  [Update Pinna](https://elsbrock.github.io/pinna/docs/update/#before-you-begin).
- With a Kaeru from before 22 October 2026, an update leaves the kernel as it
  is. Install the boot image over fastboot.

## Highlights

### A voice satellite for Home Assistant

Home Assistant adds the satellite over the ESPHome API, with its key for an
encrypted connection. The wake word runs on the satellite: up to two at a
time, chosen in Home Assistant or on the dashboard's **Voice & sound** page.
The ring shows Listening, Thinking and Speaking and points at the person
talking. The buttons, timers and a notification light are entities in Home
Assistant.

### Standalone mode

Without Home Assistant, the satellite answers requests itself, with the speech
services and the language model or AI agent you choose: OpenAI-compatible
servers, Anthropic, Gemini and ElevenLabs. The satellite has timers, the
weather and internet radio of its own, and the language model can use your MCP
servers.
Set it up on the dashboard's **Assistant** page. Home Assistant switches modes
with the **Voice mode** select.

### Set up and manage it from the dashboard

The satellite serves a dashboard on your network. You need no app, no account
and no Home Assistant. It sets the Wi-Fi, the name, the time zone, the volume
and the wake words, turns AirPlay and Bluetooth on or off, shows the logs,
restarts and resets the satellite, and switches to other satellites it finds
on your network.

### Updates that go back when they fail

The satellite looks for releases on its channel: **Updates** is `stable`,
`beta` or `off`. A new release shows on the dashboard's **System** page and as
the **Firmware** update in Home Assistant, and installs when you select
Install. It goes into the second slot, and if it doesn't start, the satellite
goes back to the version before. When a start fails, the ring shows how far it
got, and rescue mode lets you recover the satellite.

## New in Home Assistant

- **LED ring** (light)
- **Volume**, **Reply volume**, **Wake word sensitivity** and
  **LED brightness** (number)
- **Wake word**, **Listening direction**, **AirPlay**, **Bluetooth audio**,
  **Bluetooth pairing**, **Wake word calibration**, **Wake sound** and
  **Notification** (switch)
- **Cancel request** (button)
- **Volume up button**, **Volume down button**, **Microphone mute button** and
  **Action button** (event): press, long press and double press
- **Voice assistant**, **Playback**, **Timer** and **Notification** (event)
- **Last wake word** (sensor)
- **Media player** (media player): URLs and announcements
- **Updates** and **Voice mode** (select)
- **Firmware** (update)

## Other changes

- AirPlay and Bluetooth play music from phones and computers. The dashboard
  shows what plays and where it comes from.
- The Echo (2nd gen) runs Pinna as well as the Echo Dot (2nd gen).
- On the Echo Dot (2nd gen), echo cancellation lowers the satellite's own music
  by 23 to 31 dB in what the microphones hear.
- Quiet speech from across a room wakes the satellite.
- The wake word interrupts any request and stops a ringing timer alarm.
- **Wake word calibration** tunes the sensitivity with the volume buttons.
- The satellite serves metrics for Prometheus on `/metrics`.
