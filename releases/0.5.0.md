# Pinna 0.5.0

Satellites in standalone mode can form a group that passes messages and acts on each other. You can add your own wake words, play music from Music Assistant over Sendspin, and protect the dashboard with a password.

## Before you update

- **The Wi-Fi driver changed.** The setup hotspot needs a part of the MT8163
  Wi-Fi driver that 0.3.0 left out. The update's health check doesn't need
  Wi-Fi: if the satellite doesn't join your network after the update, it
  stays on the new version, and you reach it only over USB. Keep the cable at
  hand, and see [Recovery](https://getpinna.dev/docs/recovery/).
- **The dashboard offers a password.** The first time you open it after the
  update, it asks you to set one or to leave it open. Leaving it open changes
  nothing. Before you set one: Prometheus and agents that call `/metrics` or
  `/mcp` need tokens, which you can create first. If you later go back to
  0.3.0, that version doesn't ask for the password.
- **Pinna finds the hardware in a new way.** The code that finds the ring,
  the buttons, the microphone switch and the sound card was rewritten to read
  one description of each Echo. Nothing should change, but this is tested in
  the virtual machine, not yet on an Echo. If the ring, a button or the sound
  stops working after the update, go back to 0.3.0, as
  [Go back to an older version](https://getpinna.dev/docs/update/#go-back-to-an-older-version)
  describes.

## Highlights

### Satellite groups

Satellites in standalone mode on the same subnet can form a satellite group.
Ask one to pass a message, such as "tell the kitchen that dinner is ready",
and the satellite in the kitchen says it in its own voice. A satellite that is
busy holds up to 5 messages and plays them once it is Idle. The brain can also
act on another member by its name: "turn the kitchen down", "set a 10 minute
timer in the office" or "stop the music in the hall". Set the group up on each
satellite's dashboard under **Assistant > Satellite group**, where **Group
messages** and **Control from the group** turn either part off for that
satellite. If two satellites of a group hear the wake word, both still answer:
choosing one is planned. See
[Satellite groups](https://getpinna.dev/docs/satellite-groups/).

### Add your own wake word

Open **Voice & sound > Wake words** to add up to eight wake words of your own:
a phrase and its openWakeWord model, an `.onnx` or `.tflite` file of at most
2 MiB. The satellite tests the model in a process of its own before it keeps
it, and if it refuses the model, the page says why. Pinna can't run
microWakeWord models, and says so when you add one. Choose the new word like
the built-in ones, on **Voice & sound** or in Home Assistant. See
[Add your own wake word](https://getpinna.dev/docs/wake-words/#add-your-own-wake-word).

### Play music with Sendspin

Turn on **Sendspin** under **Voice & sound** > **Media sources**, or with the
**Sendspin** switch in Home Assistant. Music Assistant 2.10.7 or later then
lists the satellite as a player and plays to it in step with the other
Sendspin speakers of a group. The wake word keeps working, and replies,
announcements and timer alarms play over the music. Sendspin is experimental
and off until you turn it on. It is tested against Music Assistant's server on
a computer, not yet on an Echo. See
[Play music with Sendspin](https://getpinna.dev/docs/sendspin/).

### A password for the dashboard

To set one, open **System > Password and tokens**, select **Set a password…**,
and press **Action** on the satellite when the ring circles white. With a
password, the dashboard, the control API and the log need you to be signed in,
and the metrics and `/mcp` need
[tokens](https://getpinna.dev/docs/dashboard/#tokens-for-prometheus-and-agents).
It also closes the ESPHome API to a Home Assistant that has no key for the
satellite yet, and group joins, until you open them. The password travels
unencrypted, so use one that you use nowhere else. See
[Who can use the dashboard](https://getpinna.dev/docs/dashboard/#who-can-use-the-dashboard).

## New in Home Assistant

- **Group messages** (switch): whether the satellite takes messages from the
  other satellites of its group, for example turned off by an automation at
  night.
- **Sendspin** (switch): turns the Sendspin player on or off.
- **Install updates** (select): `Manually` or `Automatically`.
- **Install window start** and **Install window end** (time): when automatic
  installs may happen, in the satellite's time zone.
- The **Wake word** and **Wake word 2** selectors list the words you added.

## Other changes

- Updates can install by themselves in a window you choose, 03:00 to 05:00
  unless you change it: set **Install updates** to **Automatically** on
  **System > Updates**. The satellite restarts only when it is Idle, and
  **Manually** stays the default. See
  [Install updates automatically](https://getpinna.dev/docs/update/#install-updates-automatically).
- A satellite with no saved Wi-Fi network opens a setup hotspot, **Pinna
  setup** followed by its hostname, with the password `pinnasetup`. Join it
  with your phone and choose your network. It is tested in the virtual
  machine, not yet on an Echo. See
  [Connect to Wi-Fi](https://getpinna.dev/docs/flash/#connect-to-wi-fi).
- In standalone mode, ask "is there an update?": the satellite says which
  version it runs and checks for a new one. It installs one only once you turn
  on **Install update** under **Assistant** > **Tools**. See
  [Ask the satellite to update](https://getpinna.dev/docs/update/#ask-the-satellite-to-update).
- **System > Record audio** records up to 30 seconds of what the satellite
  hears, for a developer to see why the wake word or the echo cancellation
  misbehaves. It needs a dashboard password and a press of **Action**. See
  [Record what the satellite hears](https://getpinna.dev/docs/troubleshooting/#record-what-the-satellite-hears).
- **Copy for an issue** on **System > Logs** copies the version, the model, how
  the last start ended and the log, without addresses or Wi-Fi names. See
  [Report a problem](https://getpinna.dev/docs/troubleshooting/#report-a-problem).
- The log fills the window and follows the newest line while you are at the
  bottom.
- **System** opens pages for **Updates**, **Password and tokens** and
  **Logs**, and the ring's settings have their own page under **Voice &
  sound > Ring**.
- The dashboard shows Pinna's new mark.
- The satellite reports its release version to Home Assistant, in its logs
  and in `--version`, instead of 0.1.0.
- The log names the level, the automatic gain and the score of each wake word.

## Fixed

- Two changes close together, such as a new name and a new key from Home
  Assistant, no longer leave the older of them in the satellite's mDNS record.
- Rescue mode and the dashboard link to the docs at getpinna.dev.
