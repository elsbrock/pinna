# Pinna 0.4.0-beta.1

Satellites in standalone mode can form a group: ask one to pass a message to another, or to act on another by its name. You can also ask a satellite which version it runs, check for updates and, once you allow it, install one.

## Before you update

This is a beta: it goes to satellites whose **Updates** setting is `beta`.

## Highlights

### Pass messages between satellites

Satellites on the same subnet can form a satellite group. Ask one to pass a
message, such as "tell the kitchen that dinner is ready", and the satellite in
the kitchen says "Message from Hall: dinner is ready." in its own voice. A
satellite that is busy holds up to 5 messages and plays them once it is idle;
the dashboard's **Overview** shows how many it holds. Set the group up on each
satellite's dashboard under **Assistant** > **Satellite group**, as
[Pass messages between satellites](https://getpinna.dev/docs/standalone/#pass-messages-between-satellites)
describes. To stop a satellite from taking messages, turn off **Group
messages**, on its dashboard or in Home Assistant. If two satellites of a group
hear the wake word, both still answer: choosing one is planned.

### Control another satellite by its name

In a satellite group, the brain can act on another member: "turn the kitchen
down", "set a 10 minute timer in the office" or "stop the music in the hall".
It covers the volume, the reply volume, the ring light, the media player, the
radio, timers, and stopping a request, an alarm or a message. A timer set on
another satellite rings there. To stop the others from acting on a satellite,
turn off **Control from the group** under **Assistant** on its dashboard. See
[Control another satellite](https://getpinna.dev/docs/standalone/#control-another-satellite).

### Ask the satellite to update

In standalone mode, ask "is there an update?": the satellite says which
version it runs and checks for a new one. It can also install the release it
found and restart the next time it is Idle, but only once you turn on
**Install update** under **Assistant** > **Tools**, which starts off. See
[Ask the satellite to update](https://getpinna.dev/docs/update/#ask-the-satellite-to-update).

## New in Home Assistant

- **Group messages** (switch): whether the satellite takes messages from the
  other satellites of its group, for example turned off by an automation at
  night.

## Other changes

- The ring's settings have their own page: **Voice & sound** > **Ring**.
- The satellite reports its release version to Home Assistant, in its logs
  and in `--version`, instead of 0.1.0.
- The log names the level, the automatic gain and the score of each wake word.

## Fixed

- Two changes close together, such as a new name and a new key from Home
  Assistant, no longer leave the older of them in the satellite's mDNS record.
- Rescue mode and the dashboard link to the docs at getpinna.dev.

## All changes

Changes since 0.3.0.

### New

- The ring settings have their own page under Voice & sound (dashboard)
- The brain tells the version and update state and checks for updates (pinnad)
- The brain installs an update once the owner turns the tool on (pinnad)
- Binaries report the release version instead of 0.1.0
- The capture loop logs each wake word's level before the gain (pinnad)
- PeerService carries signed calls between satellites in a group (proto)
- Group membership rules and signatures (ADR 0026) (pinnad)
- The mDNS record can carry the satellite's group (pinna-esphome)
- The search reads each satellite's group and counts its searches (pinnad)
- The satellite group is saved in standalone.toml with its key in secrets.toml (pinnad)
- The dashboard reads the satellite group and its candidates (pinnad)
- The mDNS record carries the satellite's group from boot (pinnad)
- A group's signature names the call it was made for (pinnad)
- The dashboard adds and removes satellite group members through PeerService (pinnad)
- Members exchange the group's list after each search and leave a group that ended (pinnad)
- Group messages is a setting and a Home Assistant switch (pinnad)
- A member says a message from another member, or holds it until it is free (pinnad)
- The brain sends a message to a member or to all of them (pinnad)
- The brain's context names the other members of the satellite group (pinnad)
- A member runs a tool call from the group as its own /mcp would (pinnad)
- The brain acts on another member's satellite by its name (pinnad)
- The Assistant page sets up the satellite group (dashboard)
- The Overview shows how many messages are held (dashboard)

### Fixed

- The mDNS record ends up as the latest of two changes close together (pinnad)
- The docs links point to getpinna.dev
